**The control saturates, and the prompt change is why.** It answers all 16 tasks correctly at component mean 1.000, against 13 of 16 for the same stratum in the sweep. Run-to-run noise does not account for it: `BA-01`, `BA-12`, and `BA-15` were failed by the control in *every* stratum of the sweep — luna, terra, and sol alike — and by every earlier execution's control; all three pass here. Three tasks that were stable failures across three independent strata do not flip together by chance. They flip because what they tested was undeclared population semantics, and the prompt now declares them. **A large part of the Codex `C_strict` accuracy gain published above was the treatment arm compensating for prompt ambiguity the control had to guess at.** With the ambiguity moved into the prompt for all three arms, that compensation has nothing left to buy, and this stratum's strict-arm accuracy gain inverts from +6.2 points to −25.0.
